# Popular Live Data Safety Worksheet

Use this as the factual basis for the Play Console Data safety form. Confirm each answer against production operations before submitting.

## Collection

Popular Live collects or processes these categories:

- Personal information: name, email address, phone number, user ID, profile biography, and profile image.
- Photos and videos: profile media, uploaded media, and live video content.
- Audio: live-room audio and microphone content.
- Messages: direct messages and live-room chat.
- Location: approximate or precise location when regional or nearby features are used.
- Financial activity: diamond top-ups, gifts, bean balances, withdrawal requests, and transaction history. The app does not collect payment-card numbers directly.
- App activity: follows, room participation, gifts, mini-game activity, and feature interactions.
- Device or other identifiers: app/device identifier, IP address, device model, and operating-system information.
- App information and performance: error reports, diagnostics, and crash context.

## Purposes

- App functionality, account management, communication, personalization, analytics, fraud prevention, security, compliance, and developer communications/support.
- Data is encrypted in transit.
- Users can request deletion in Settings > Delete Account or at the public deletion URL.

## Service Providers

- Supabase: authentication, database, storage, and server functions.
- Agora: real-time audio and video delivery.
- Google: optional Google Sign-In and Play distribution services.

The operator should confirm whether Google Play's current definition treats each provider transfer as service-provider processing or as sharing before submitting the form.

## Declarations

- No advertising SDK was identified in the current app dependencies.
- The app is intended for adults aged 18 and older.
- User-generated content, social interaction, virtual gifts, and simulated/social mini-games must be disclosed in App content and the content-rating questionnaire.
- Account deletion anonymizes profile data and retains limited transaction, gift, game, safety, and fraud-prevention records for legitimate operational or legal purposes.
