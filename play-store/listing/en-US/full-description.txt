Popular Live is a social live-streaming community where adults can meet, talk, and share moments in real time.

Join live video broadcasts or audio rooms, follow creators, chat with the community, and express your support with virtual gifts. Hosts can build communities, review their activity, and manage their Popular Live experience from one place.

Features include:

• Live video broadcasts and multi-guest audio rooms
• Real-time chat, reactions, and virtual gifts
• Profiles, follows, badges, and membership levels
• Host tools, activity statistics, and agency features
• Social mini-games played with in-app virtual items
• Account safety, privacy controls, and in-app account deletion

Popular Live is intended for users aged 18 and older. Community content is user-generated. Please use the reporting and blocking tools when necessary and follow the community rules.
