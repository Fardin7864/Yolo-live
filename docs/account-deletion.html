Delete Your Popular Live Account

Delete inside the app

  1. Open Popular Live and sign in.
  2. Open Profile, then Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account and confirm Delete Permanently.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email carelive785@gmail.com from the email associated with your account. Include your Popular Live user ID. We may ask you to verify account ownership.

What is deleted

Your profile information is removed or anonymized, open requests are cancelled, memberships are released, notifications are disabled, and the account can no longer be used.

What may be retained

Limited transaction, gift, game, safety, and fraud-prevention records may be retained where needed for legal, accounting, security, or dispute-resolution purposes. They are restricted and deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.
