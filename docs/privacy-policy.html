Popular Live Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This policy explains how the Popular Live team collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects information when you use the Popular Live mobile application.

Information we collect

We may collect account information such as your name, phone number, email address, user ID, profile image, and biography; user content such as live video or audio, photos, and chat messages; device and network information such as IP address, device identifier, operating system, device model, and diagnostics; location information when you use regional or nearby features; and wallet or transaction information such as diamond top-ups, gifts, bean balances, and withdrawal requests.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, operate live audio and video rooms, deliver messages and gifts, provide regional features, process virtual transactions and payout requests, provide support, diagnose errors, enforce safety rules, prevent fraud, and improve the service.

Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal information. We use service providers including Supabase for authentication, database, and storage services; Agora for live audio and video delivery; and Google for optional sign-in. These providers process information as needed to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users, or to investigate abuse and fraud.

Permissions

Popular Live may request camera and microphone access for live rooms, media access for profile and shared content, and location access for regional or nearby features. You can manage these permissions in Android settings.

Retention and account deletion

You can delete your account in the app through Settings > Delete Account or follow the instructions on our account deletion page. Deletion removes or anonymizes profile information and disables the account. Limited transaction, gift, game, safety, and fraud-prevention records may be retained where required for legal, accounting, security, or dispute-resolution purposes. Retained records are restricted and are deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

Security

We use encrypted network connections, access controls, and monitoring to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Popular Live is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly permit children to create accounts.
